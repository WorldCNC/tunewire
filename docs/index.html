Questions

Good to know

Is Tunewire really free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads, no premium tier. It’s open source on GitHub.

Where does the music come from?

From YouTube Music, SoundCloud and YouTube, plus your own files. Spotify songs play from the same recording elsewhere, or from Spotify itself if you have Premium.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything plays without one. Sign in with Discord only if you want friends, sharing, collaborative playlists and listening together.

What do friends see?

Only what you allow. People need your approval to follow you, and sharing what you play or your stats is off until you turn it on.

Can I move my library?

Yes. One backup file holds your playlists, Liked Songs, history and stats, and restores on any computer.
